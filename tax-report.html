Reporting Period
Basis: cash — revenue and tax are attributed to the month payment was actually received, not the invoice date. Only signed/approved invoices are included (unsigned quotes and declined proposals are excluded). Styling With Jas invoices only — GS Photography has its own separate numbering and isn't included here. Category tags (Retailing vs. Service & Other) and B&O amounts are a best-effort allocation per the method documented in TAX_METHODOLOGY.md, not a CPA-certified classification — please have this reviewed before filing. Card processing fees are a benchmark estimate (2.9% + $0.30 per card payment) — this app has no live Stripe fee feed, so check your Stripe dashboard for actuals.

This report is also emailed automatically to info@stylingwithjas.com on the 1st of every month, covering the month that just ended. The button above triggers that exact same email on demand, for testing.
