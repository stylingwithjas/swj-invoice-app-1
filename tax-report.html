Tax Report
Reporting Period
Basis: cash — revenue and tax are attributed to the month payment was actually received, not the invoice date. Every Styling With Jas staging job with a payment received this month is included — signature status is shown as a flag on each line, not a filter; an unsigned-but-paid job is counted in full and flagged for paperwork follow-up, never dropped. A job with no payment event this period (a draft, an open quote, a declined/closed-lost proposal) stays out because it has no payment, not because of its signature. Styling With Jas invoices only — GS Photography has its own separate numbering and isn't included here. Category tags (Retailing vs. Service & Other) and B&O amounts are a best-effort allocation per the method documented in TAX_METHODOLOGY.md, not a CPA-certified classification — please have this reviewed before filing. Card processing fees are a benchmark estimate (2.9% + $0.30 per card payment) — this app has no live Stripe fee feed, so check your Stripe dashboard for actuals.

This report, the monthly audit packet (invoices + signed-contract links + payments + reconciliation + DOR filing slot), and the full website backup (every invoice — including drafts and GS Photography — plus tasks, time entries, and employee records, excluding any credential) are all emailed automatically to info@stylingwithjas.com on the 1st of every month, covering the month that just ended — three emails from the same monthly run, also saved to Google Drive once connected. The buttons above trigger each one on demand, for testing.
