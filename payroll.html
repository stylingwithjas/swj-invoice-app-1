Manual Override
Add a job the automatic rules missed, remove one with its "Remove from budget" button, or flip a wrong one-off flag. This never deletes anything — it only changes what shows for this month.
Reconciliation
Revenue is cash-basis (signed invoices, payment-received month — same basis as the Tax Report). Mover pay is actual, from logged hours this month. Truck and warehouse costs use the editable assumptions above, since this app doesn't track per-job truck receipts yet — correct the defaults above if your actual costs differ. Tax set-aside reuses the Tax Report's B&O + sales-tax method (not CPA-certified — see TAX_METHODOLOGY.md). "Jasmine keep (actual net)" is real profit; "Jasmine keep (target)" is a benchmark line only — the two are never blended, and the target is capped at actual profit so it can never exceed what the month really made. Inventory (shopping budget) is derived, never entered: whatever is left after costs and the keep target. A month with unreconciled revenue is flagged, not presented as final. Viewing this page never changes a stored record — only the Manual Override controls write anything.
