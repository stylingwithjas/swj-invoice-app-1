Manual Override
Add a job the automatic rules missed, remove one with its "Remove from budget" button, or flip a wrong one-off flag. This never deletes anything — it only changes what shows for this month.
Collected receipts use payment-received dates, including unsigned paid invoices and paid extensions, excluding test charges. They include collected sales tax. Planning exclusions do not change recorded receipts. Checking deposits and transfers are excluded from revenue. Mover pay is actual, from logged hours this month. Truck and warehouse costs use the editable assumptions above, since this app doesn't track per-job truck receipts yet — correct the defaults above if your actual costs differ. Tax set-aside reuses the Tax Report's B&O + sales-tax method (not CPA-certified — see TAX_METHODOLOGY.md). "Jasmine keep (actual net)" is the existing planning allocation result, including estimated costs, not accounting profit; "Jasmine keep (target)" is a benchmark line only — the two are never blended, and the target is capped at actual profit so it can never exceed what the month really made. Inventory (shopping budget) is derived, never entered: whatever is left after costs and the keep target. A month with unreconciled revenue is flagged, not presented as final. Viewing this page never changes a stored record — only the Manual Override controls write anything.
