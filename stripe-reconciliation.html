Coverage, matching rules & limitations

    Matching order: (1) metadata + amount to the cent, (2) amount + date (±7 days) + client, all three required, (3) otherwise unmatched. Never auto-matched on one signal alone — an ambiguous charge (more than one equally-qualifying candidate) is never auto-picked either.

    Findings here are questions for owner/CPA review. This report never updates a record, issues a charge or refund, or sends anything. GS Photography is excluded entirely.
